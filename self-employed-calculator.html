Critical Disclaimer - Self-Employed Income

This calculator is for preliminary estimation only. Self-employed income qualification requires:

  • Complete signed federal tax returns (typically 2 years, including all schedules)
  • Detailed review by a qualified underwriter and/or tax professional
  • Verification of business continuance and stability
  • Analysis of year-over-year trends (declining income may not qualify at average)
  • Additional documentation may be required (1099s, profit/loss statements, business licenses)

This calculator cannot replace professional underwriting analysis. Always consult with a licensed mortgage professional for accurate qualification assessment.

Additional Requirements: Self-employed borrowers must provide complete signed federal tax returns with all schedules for typically the most recent 2 years. If income is declining year-over-year, underwriters may use only the most recent year or may not approve the loan depending on trend analysis and compensating factors. CPA letters, year-to-date profit/loss statements, and business license verification may be required. This calculator uses standard income calculation methods but cannot account for all underwriting scenarios, lender overlays, or loan program requirements.
