Working with Kristy Flach

I built Agent Edge because I believe the best partnerships start with value — not promises, not obligations, just real tools that help you do your job better.

I've spent two decades in the mortgage industry. I started as a senior underwriter, where I learned every rule, guideline, and exception that determines whether a loan gets approved or denied. I earned my real estate license in Washington State. I owned and operated a loan signing company. I've sat on every side of the closing table.

That 360-degree experience is what I bring to you — and it's exactly what I poured into Agent Edge.

I'm always a phone call away — that doesn't change. But sometimes it's 10pm and you just need a quick answer, or you're sitting across from a client and want to pull up a comparison on the spot, or you're prepping for an open house and need materials ready now. Agent Edge is there for those moments. It's an extension of me — available whenever you need it, working alongside the direct support you already get.

And here's what your clients see — an agent who has answers. Not one who says "let me check with my lender and get back to you." When you can pull up a payment comparison while you're still sitting at the kitchen table, or walk a seller through a buydown strategy with real numbers on the spot, that changes how they see you. You become the agent who just knows. That's the edge.

Everything on Agent Edge is free. There's no catch, no subscription, no hidden fees. Here's what you get:

Morning Briefings — real-time market intelligence delivered to you daily. What's happening with rates, what's moving the market, and why it matters to your clients. Plus a client-friendly version you can share directly — keeping you top of mind and positioned as the expert they trust.

A complete marketing library — over 40 professionally designed, co-branded flyers covering buyers, sellers, financing, construction, education, and open house materials. Self-serve PDF generators with built-in mortgage calculators so you can create property-specific flyers on the spot. Seven custom single property website templates you can deploy for any listing.

Social media tools — a library of ready-to-post content organized by category, an AI-powered custom post generator, a professional image library, and direct publishing to Facebook with quick-share tools for Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube.

Client-ready reports — seven analytical reports that help your clients understand their buying power, home equity, investment potential, and more. These aren't generic handouts — they're data-driven tools that make you look like the expert you are.

Decision tools — a Scenario Desk where you can search lending guidelines across five major agencies and submit complex scenarios for expert review and a callback. A Market Pulse tracker with interactive home value trends across all 50 states. A Seller Strategies calculator that shows clients smarter alternatives to price reductions.

Educational resources your clients can use — detailed loan program guides for Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, Non-Conforming, Non-Qualifying, and Reverse Mortgages. A Credit Score Simulator. Financial calculators. All written in plain English so your clients can actually understand what they're getting into.

A credit analysis pipeline — submit client credit scenarios for review and guidance.

Everything is co-branded with your name and information. When your clients see these materials, they see you — backed by a lending team that knows what they're doing.

I built this because strong agents deserve strong support. When you succeed, I succeed. It's that simple.
