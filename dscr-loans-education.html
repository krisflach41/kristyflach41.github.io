What is a DSCR Loan?

A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan is an investment property mortgage that qualifies borrowers based on the property's rental income rather than the borrower's personal income, W-2s, or tax returns. This makes it ideal for real estate investors who want to scale their portfolios without traditional income verification.

How DSCR Works

The Debt Service Coverage Ratio measures whether a rental property generates enough income to cover its monthly debt obligations (mortgage payment, taxes, insurance, HOA).

DSCR = Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly PITIA Payment

Example: If a property generates $3,000/month in rent and the total PITIA payment is $2,400:

DSCR = $3,000 ÷ $2,400 = 1.25

A DSCR of 1.25 means the property generates 25% more income than needed to cover the debt.

Understanding DSCR Ratios

  • DSCR > 1.0: Property generates MORE income than needed (positive cash flow)
  • DSCR = 1.0: Property generates EXACTLY enough to cover debt (break-even)
  • DSCR < 1.0: Property generates LESS than debt obligations (negative cash flow - higher risk)

Most lenders prefer DSCR of 1.25 or higher for best terms, though many now accept 1.0 or lower with compensating factors.

Major Benefits

  • No personal income verification required (no W-2s, tax returns, pay stubs)
  • Qualify based solely on property's rental income
  • Perfect for self-employed investors with tax write-offs
  • No limit on number of properties you can finance
  • Can be held in LLC or personal name
  • Faster approval process than conventional loans
  • Great for portfolio growth and scaling
  • Works for long-term and short-term rentals (Airbnb)

Typical Loan Terms

  • Interest Rates: 6.5% - 8.0% (typically 0.5-1.5% higher than conventional)
  • Loan Amounts: Up to $3-5 million (lender dependent)
  • Loan Terms: 30-year fixed most common
  • LTV: Maximum 75-80%
  • Prepayment Penalties: Common (typically 1-5 years)
  • Closing Time: 21-30 days typically

Eligible Property Types

  • Single-family homes (1-4 units)
  • Condos and townhomes
  • Multi-family properties
  • Short-term rentals (Airbnb, VRBO)
  • Long-term rental properties

Important: Property must be investment/rental property. DSCR loans CANNOT be used for primary residences or owner-occupied properties.

Who Should Consider a DSCR Loan?

DSCR loans are perfect for investors who:

  • Are self-employed with significant tax write-offs (low reported income)
  • Own multiple rental properties and want to expand
  • Don't want to provide extensive personal financial documentation
  • Have properties with strong rental income
  • Want to scale portfolio quickly
  • Hold properties in LLCs
  • Need flexible qualification requirements

How Rental Income is Calculated

Lenders use the LESSER of:

  • Actual Lease Agreement: Current signed lease showing monthly rent
  • Appraiser's Market Rent: Form 1007 comparable rent schedule

Some lenders accept 2-3 month rental history in lieu of appraisal if documented.

DSCR vs. Conventional Investment Loans

DSCR Loans:

  • No personal income verification
  • No limit on number of properties
  • Can be in LLC name
  • Higher rates (6.5-8%)
  • Faster approval

Conventional Investment Loans:

  • Requires W-2s, tax returns, pay stubs
  • Limited to 10 financed properties
  • Must be in personal name
  • Lower rates (6-7%)
  • Stricter DTI requirements

Common Use Cases

  • Portfolio Expansion: Finance multiple properties without personal income limits
  • Cash-Out Refinance: Pull equity from existing rentals to fund new purchases
  • Short-Term Rentals: Finance Airbnb/VRBO properties based on rental projections
  • LLC Ownership: Hold properties in LLC while still qualifying for financing
  • Self-Employed Investors: Qualify without showing full tax returns

Important Considerations

  • Rates are higher than conventional loans (0.5-1.5% premium)
  • Prepayment penalties are common (1-5 years)
  • Larger down payments required (20-25%)
  • Must have cash reserves (6-12 months typical)
  • Property must be investment/rental (not owner-occupied)
  • Some lenders charge higher fees

Improving Your DSCR

To qualify for better terms:

  • Increase rental income (raise rents to market rate)
  • Make larger down payment (reduces monthly debt service)
  • Reduce property expenses
  • Minimize vacancies
  • Improve credit score (720+ gets best rates)
  • Increase cash reserves
