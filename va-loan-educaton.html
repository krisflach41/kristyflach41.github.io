VA Loans

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs Home Loans

Important Disclaimer The loan programs presented here represent the most commonly used mortgage products available, but this is not a comprehensive list of all financing options. Guidelines are subject to change by government agencies (VA) and lender overlays at any time. This information is provided as an educational tool only. What is a VA Loan?

VA loans are government-backed mortgages available to active-duty service members, veterans, and eligible surviving spouses. These loans are guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, allowing lenders to offer exceptional terms without requiring a down payment or monthly mortgage insurance.

Key Benefits

Down Payment
$0 (Zero down payment required)
Mortgage Insurance
None (No PMI required)
Credit Score
No VA minimum (lender overlays typically 580-620)
Loan Limits
No limit with full entitlement

VA Funding Fee

Most borrowers pay a one-time VA funding fee:

Down Payment First-Time Use Subsequent Use
0% down 2.15% 3.30%
5% - 9.99% down 1.50% 1.50%
10%+ down 1.25% 1.25%

Funding Fee Exemptions: Veterans receiving VA disability compensation are exempt from the funding fee.

Eligibility Requirements

You must obtain a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) and meet service requirements:

  • Active-duty service members (90+ days during wartime, 181+ days during peacetime)
  • Veterans who served and were honorably discharged
  • National Guard/Reserve members (6+ years of service)
  • Surviving spouses of veterans who died in service or from service-connected disabilities

Major Benefits

  • Zero down payment (100% financing available)
  • No monthly mortgage insurance (PMI/MIP)
  • Competitive interest rates (typically lower than conventional)
  • Lenient credit requirements
  • No prepayment penalties
  • Limited closing costs (seller can pay up to 4%)
  • Loans are assumable (transferable to buyers)
  • Can be used multiple times

Property Requirements

The property must:

  • Be your primary residence
  • Meet VA minimum property requirements (safety, soundness, sanitation)
  • Pass a VA appraisal
  • Be a one-to-four unit home, townhouse, or VA-approved condo

Who Should Consider a VA Loan?

If you're eligible for VA benefits, this is almost always your best option because:

  • No down payment saves you tens of thousands upfront
  • No monthly mortgage insurance saves $100-300+ per month
  • Lower interest rates save thousands over the life of the loan
  • More flexible qualification standards than conventional loans

VA Loan vs. Other Options

Compared to FHA: VA loans have no upfront or monthly mortgage insurance, while FHA requires both. VA also allows 100% financing vs. FHA's 3.5% minimum down.

Compared to Conventional: VA loans require no down payment and no mortgage insurance, while conventional loans require 3-20% down and PMI if less than 20% down.
