What is an FHA Loan?

An FHA loan is a mortgage insured by the Federal Housing Administration. These loans are designed to help low-to-moderate income borrowers become homeowners, particularly first-time buyers. The government insurance protects lenders, allowing them to offer more flexible qualification requirements.

Key Requirements (2026)

Minimum Credit Score
580 for 3.5% down
500-579 requires 10% down
Down Payment
3.5% (with 580+ credit score)
10% (with 500-579 credit score)
Debt-to-Income Ratio
Up to 50% (43% or lower preferred)
Loan Limits (2026)
$541,287 (most counties)
$1,249,125 (high-cost areas)

Mortgage Insurance (MIP)

FHA loans require two types of mortgage insurance:

  • Upfront MIP: 1.75% of loan amount (can be rolled into loan)
  • Annual MIP: Typically 0.55% of loan amount, paid monthly
  • Duration: Life of loan if you put down less than 10%
  • Duration: 11 years if you put down 10% or more

Note: MIP cannot be canceled like PMI on conventional loans unless you refinance.

Benefits

  • Low down payment (as low as 3.5%)
  • Flexible credit requirements (scores as low as 580)
  • Gift funds allowed for down payment and closing costs
  • Seller can contribute up to 6% toward closing costs
  • Ideal for first-time homebuyers
  • More lenient debt-to-income ratios
  • Assumable loans (transferable to future buyers)

Who Should Consider an FHA Loan?

FHA loans work best for borrowers who have:

  • Credit scores below 680
  • Less than 5% saved for down payment
  • Higher debt-to-income ratios (above 43%)
  • Recent credit challenges (bankruptcy 2+ years ago, foreclosure 3+ years ago)
  • Limited financial history as first-time buyers

Property Requirements

The property must:

  • Be your primary residence (must move in within 60 days)
  • Meet FHA minimum property standards for safety and soundness
  • Pass an FHA appraisal
  • Be a one-to-four unit home or FHA-approved condo

FHA vs. Conventional

Choose FHA if: You have a lower credit score (below 680), limited down payment savings (less than 5%), or need more flexible qualification standards.

Choose Conventional if: You have a credit score above 720, can put down 5-20%, and want to cancel mortgage insurance once you reach 20% equity.

Closing Costs

FHA closing costs typically range from 3-6% of the purchase price. The good news? Sellers can contribute up to 6% toward your closing costs, and you can use gift funds from family members to cover down payment and closing expenses.
