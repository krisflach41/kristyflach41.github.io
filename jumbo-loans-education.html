What is a Jumbo Loan?

A jumbo loan is a mortgage that exceeds the conforming loan limits set by the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA). Because these loans exceed Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac limits, they're considered "non-conforming" and carry stricter qualification requirements to offset the lender's increased risk.

Stricter Qualification Standards

Because jumbo loans aren't backed by government agencies, lenders require:

  • Higher Credit Scores: Typically 700+ minimum, with 720-740+ for the best rates and terms
  • Larger Down Payments: Usually 10-20%, with 20%+ needed for the most competitive rates
  • Lower DTI Ratios: Preferably 36-43%, with strong compensating factors if higher
  • Substantial Cash Reserves: 6-12 months of mortgage payments in liquid assets after closing
  • Excellent Documentation: Two years of W-2s, tax returns, pay stubs, asset statements
  • Stable Income: Consistent employment history and verifiable income sources

Interest Rates

Jumbo loan rates are typically 0.25% to 0.75% higher than conforming loan rates, though this gap has narrowed in recent years. Borrowers with excellent credit (740+), large down payments (20%+), and strong financial profiles may qualify for rates competitive with conventional loans.

Cash Reserve Requirements

Lenders want assurance you can continue making payments even if your income is disrupted. Typical reserve requirements:

  • Primary Residence: 6-12 months of payments
  • Second Home: 12+ months of payments
  • Investment Property: 12-18+ months of payments
  • High Loan Amounts ($2M+): 18-24+ months of payments

Note: Reserves can include checking/savings accounts, retirement accounts (partial credit), stocks, bonds, and other liquid assets.

Who Should Consider a Jumbo Loan?

Jumbo loans are designed for borrowers who:

  • Are purchasing high-value properties exceeding conforming limits
  • Have excellent credit scores (720+)
  • Can make substantial down payments (10-20%+)
  • Have significant liquid assets and cash reserves
  • Demonstrate strong, stable income
  • Maintain low debt-to-income ratios

Property Types

Jumbo loans can be used to finance:

  • Primary residences
  • Second homes and vacation properties
  • Investment properties
  • Luxury properties and estates
  • Condos and townhomes (with additional requirements)
  • Multi-unit properties (up to 4 units)

Benefits of Jumbo Loans

  • Finance high-value properties above conforming limits
  • Competitive rates for well-qualified borrowers
  • Flexible loan terms (10, 15, 20, 30 years)
  • Available for various property types
  • No mortgage insurance with 20%+ down payment
  • Can be used multiple times

Important Considerations

  • Rates: Slightly higher than conventional loans (0.25-0.75% typically)
  • Scrutiny: More extensive underwriting and documentation requirements
  • Reserves: Must maintain significant liquid assets after closing
  • Appraisals: Often require two appraisals for large loan amounts
  • Prepayment: Some lenders may charge prepayment penalties (verify before closing)

Jumbo vs. Conforming Loans

The main difference: Jumbo loans exceed FHFA conforming limits and aren't eligible for purchase by Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac. This means lenders hold more risk, resulting in stricter qualification standards and slightly higher rates. However, for well-qualified borrowers, jumbo loans provide access to financing for high-value properties that wouldn't otherwise be available.
