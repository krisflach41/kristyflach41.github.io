Important Disclosure

Credit score changes are complex and at times counterintuitive. This tool is for educational purposes only and provides estimates based on general scoring principles. Actual results will vary based on your complete credit profile.

Important Facts

  • Paying off or settling a collection can lower your score temporarily
  • Making a payment on an old collection can restart the aging clock
  • Closing credit accounts can hurt your score significantly
  • The timing of credit actions matters — incorrect timing can cause damage
  • Every credit situation is unique and requires professional analysis
Do not take any credit actions based on this tool. Consult with your loan officer before making changes.
