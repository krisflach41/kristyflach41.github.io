Credit score changes are COMPLEX and sometimes counterintuitive. This tool is for EDUCATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY.

🚫 CRITICAL FACTS YOU MUST UNDERSTAND:

  • Paying off or settling a collection can LOWER your score temporarily
  • Making a payment on an old collection can restart the aging clock
  • Closing credit accounts can HURT your score significantly
  • The timing of credit actions matters - wrong timing = score damage
  • Every credit situation is unique and requires professional analysis
⚠️ DO NOT TAKE ANY CREDIT ACTIONS BASED ON THIS CALCULATOR

This simulator provides ESTIMATES based on general credit scoring principles. Actual results will vary significantly based on your complete credit profile, which includes factors this calculator cannot account for.
