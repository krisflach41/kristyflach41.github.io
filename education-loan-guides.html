Loan Program Guides

Comprehensive guides covering every major loan program with current data and requirements

🏛️

Conventional Loans

Fixed and adjustable-rate mortgages backed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. 3% down options available.
🏠

FHA Loans

Government-insured loans with 3.5% down payment and flexible credit requirements for first-time buyers.
🎖️

VA Loans

Zero down payment loans for eligible veterans, active duty service members, and surviving spouses.
🌾

USDA Loans

Zero down payment loans for rural and suburban properties with income limits and location requirements.
💎

Jumbo Loans

Non-conforming loans for high-value properties exceeding conventional loan limits ($832,750+).
🔄

Reverse Mortgages

Home Equity Conversion Mortgages (HECM) for homeowners 62+ to access equity without monthly payments.
📊

DSCR Loans

Investment property loans that qualify borrowers based on rental income instead of personal income.
