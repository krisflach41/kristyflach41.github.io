Down Payment Assistance Programs

Explore Grants, Loans & Programs to Help You Buy a Home

💰 Good News: Thousands of Down Payment Assistance Programs Exist! Down payment assistance (DPA) programs can provide grants or low-interest loans to help cover your down payment and closing costs. Many programs are available through state housing finance agencies, local governments, and nonprofit organizations.

What is Down Payment Assistance?

Down Payment Assistance (DPA) programs help homebuyers who struggle to save enough for a down payment and closing costs. These programs can provide:

  • Grants: Free money you don't have to repay
  • Forgivable Loans: Loans forgiven after you live in the home for a certain period
  • Deferred-Payment Loans: Loans with no monthly payment until you sell or refinance
  • Low-Interest Loans: Affordable second mortgages with low rates
  • Matched Savings Programs: Programs that match money you save

Who Qualifies for DPA Programs?

Eligibility varies by program, but common requirements include:

🏠 First-Time Homebuyers

Many programs prioritize buyers who haven't owned a home in the last 3 years.

💵 Income Limits

Most programs have maximum income thresholds (often 80-120% of area median income).

📍 Location Requirements

Property must be in eligible areas (city, county, or state programs).

✅ Credit & Employment

Minimum credit scores (often 620-640) and stable employment history.

Types of Down Payment Assistance

State Housing Finance Agency (HFA) Programs:

  • Every state has an HFA offering DPA programs
  • Often combined with low-interest mortgages
  • Grants typically range from $2,500 to $15,000+
  • May require homebuyer education courses

Local City & County Programs:

  • Target specific neighborhoods or income levels
  • May offer more generous assistance amounts
  • Often have lower income limits than state programs
  • Can be combined with state and federal programs

Employer-Assisted Housing Programs:

  • Some employers offer DPA to attract/retain employees
  • Common for teachers, nurses, police, firefighters
  • May require working in specific areas or industries

Nonprofit & Community Programs:

  • Organizations like NACA, Habitat for Humanity, NeighborWorks
  • Often serve underserved communities
  • May include sweat equity or volunteer requirements

How Much Assistance Can You Get?

DPA amounts vary widely by program and location:

  • Flat Grants: $2,500 - $25,000+ (common range: $5,000 - $10,000)
  • Percentage of Purchase Price: 3% - 6% of home price
  • Percentage of Down Payment: 50% - 100% of required down payment
  • Closing Cost Assistance: $1,000 - $5,000+ for closing costs
